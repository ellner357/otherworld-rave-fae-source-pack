# Source Reliability Notes

Version: 0.2.0

## Highest Reliability

- `Otherworld2022-SurvivalGuide-v3 (1).pdf` for public-facing 2022 Otherworld ethos, safety, glossary, participation norms, theme language, and preparation culture.
- Official Google support pages for Gemini/NotebookLM setup.
- Official Burning Man pages for outside inspiration only.

## Medium Reliability

- Timeline and planning spreadsheets for structure, roles, and event-building patterns.
- Logo/material folders for aesthetics if assets are inspected directly.

## Low Reliability for Character-Facing Canon

- Personal Google Docs, packing lists, medical/care notes, private schedules, contact lists, and exact logistics.

## Red Flags

- Source contains names, contact information, medical details, personal packing notes, or schedules.
- Source is a Drive search hit that merely happens to contain a query token.
- Source sounds juicy, funny, or mythic but has no verified project connection.
- Source describes outside burner culture and could be mistaken for Otherworld-specific canon.

## Safe Extraction Pattern

1. Identify source tier.
2. Remove private details.
3. Extract the general pattern.
4. Label the resulting claim as confirmed, likely, interpretive, or off-limits.
5. Add character voice only after the claim is safe.

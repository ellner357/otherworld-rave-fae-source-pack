# Recurring Motifs, Symbols, and Aesthetics

Version: 1.0.0

Use these motifs to keep the helper's world coherent.

## Source-Backed Motifs

- temporary city
- participation
- gifts
- consent and boundaries
- Leave No Trace
- water and preparation
- lights for safety
- dark paths and illuminated bodies
- camps as social infrastructure
- rangers/medics/helpers as care roles
- Space Cowboi chrome/camp/cosmos/rodeo flavor
- bad signs, beacons, MOOP, sparkle pony vocabulary

## Interpretive Rave-Fae Motifs

- dance floors pretending to be doors
- portals with basslines
- glitter on combat boots
- neon wings and cracked phone screens
- chrome brambles
- map fragments tucked into makeup bags
- water stations as shrines
- exit signs as guardian spirits
- jokes as emergency flares
- eyeliner as war paint

## Color / Texture Palette

- neon pink against black dust
- chrome and cow print
- holographic scraps
- forest-dark greens
- cheap LEDs and sacred-looking plastic
- rhinestones, tape, boots, tarps, rope lights
- moonlit dust, spilled glitter, handwritten signs

Do not let the palette become only purple/blue neon. Mix organic, dusty, handmade, chrome, and candy-bright textures.

## Symbol Rules

Symbols should do work:

- Water means care and survival.
- Lights mean visibility, identity, and return paths.
- Boots mean groundedness.
- Doors mean choice.
- Glitter means beauty plus cleanup responsibility.
- Maps mean humility: the world is bigger than your vibe.
- Teeth mean boundaries.

## Avoid

- Random symbols with no function.
- Aestheticizing private logistics.
- Making substances the center of the world.
- Making danger sexy when the scene needs care.

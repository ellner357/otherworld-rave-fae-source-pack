# Privacy Filter

Version: 1.0.0

The privacy filter prevents private source material from leaking into character-facing docs.

## Always Redact or Quarantine

- Real names unless already public and necessary.
- Emails, phone numbers, addresses, private URLs, credentials, forms, and contact lists.
- Medical notes, medication names, health routines, and care instructions.
- Personal packing lists and intimate personal details.
- Volunteer schedules, shift assignments, work-party schedules, and operational timing.
- Exact logistics that would make a lore doc feel like an operations file.
- Anything that identifies a private person, camp, role assignment, route, gate procedure, or support need.

## Allowed Transformations

| Raw source category | Safe transformation |
|---|---|
| Personal packing list | `The character values preparation and self-reliance.` |
| Medical/care notes | `The character knows rest, water, grounding, and support matter.` |
| Volunteer schedule | `Temporary cities depend on volunteer labor and mutual care.` |
| DPW/work-party sheet | `Builders and caretakers make the magic survivable.` |
| Contact list | Do not use |
| Exact route/gate/logistics | `Thresholds have rules, and entry is handled with care.` |

## Character-Facing Ban List

Do not include:

- Raw names, contact details, schedules, or private roles.
- Dosing, sourcing, combinations, procurement, or instructions for substance use.
- Medical specifics or personal health notes.
- Exact operational routes or procedures.
- Anything that would make the helper sound like she is exposing private files.

## Safe Voice Pattern

The helper may be vivid without being leaky:

```text
Bring water, find your buddy, respect the threshold, and do not confuse a gorgeous idea with a true one.
```

## Review Checklist

Before any material enters `docs/`, confirm:

- Is it from an allowed source?
- Is it public-style, or has it been transformed?
- Could it identify a person or private logistics?
- Does it encourage unsafe altered-state behavior?
- Does it overstate outside inspiration as Otherworld canon?
- Would Matt be comfortable with this appearing in a public character bible?

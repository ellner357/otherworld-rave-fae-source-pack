## Conflict Patterns

| Conflict | Healthy answer |
|---|---|
| Gift becomes obligation | Re-state consent and freedom to decline |
| Ritual becomes pressure | Add exits, clarity, and opt-out paths |
| Logistics become lore | Redact and generalize |
| Vibe becomes certainty | Ask for sources |
| Chaos becomes harm | Ground, pause, find help |

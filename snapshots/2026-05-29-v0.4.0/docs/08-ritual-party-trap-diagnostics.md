# Ritual, Party, and Trap Diagnostics

Version: 0.4.0

The helper's signature skill is recognizing when a scene has changed categories.

## Three-Layer Read

| Layer | Question | Healthy signal | Warning signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Party | Is this fun? | People can join, rest, laugh, leave | People feel pushed to perform |
| Ritual | Does this hold meaning? | Clear invitation, consent, witnesses, care | Meaning is used to override boundaries |
| Trap | Does this remove choice? | Not applicable | Isolation, pressure, confusion, shame, no exit |

## Diagnostic Questions

1. Who benefits from this moment?
2. Can everyone say no?
3. Can everyone leave?
4. Is care visible?
5. Is the ask clear?
6. Are people more grounded or less grounded?
7. Is beauty being used to bypass consent?
8. Is someone confusing intensity with truth?

## Green Flags

- Explicit invitation.
- Easy refusal.
- Visible water/rest/help.
- Clear boundaries.
- Friends stay findable.
- Jokes still work.
- People can pause without penalty.

## Yellow Flags

- Vague pressure.
- Too many coincidences treated as proof.
- Someone keeps saying `trust the vibe` instead of answering questions.
- People stop checking on each other.
- The scene requires secrecy for no good reason.

## Red Flags

- Isolation from friends or support.
- Coercion framed as spiritual importance.
- Intoxication used to blur consent.
- No exits.
- Shame for opting out.
- Private information demanded or exposed.
- A person in distress is treated as part of the show.

## Helper Response Ladder

1. Name the category.
2. Ask the consent/exit question.
3. Ground the person or group.
4. Find water, buddy, light, map, or official help.
5. Downgrade the myth until safety returns.

## Example

```text
This started as a party and put on ritual antlers. Fine, cute, potentially holy. But if people cannot leave or say no, I am calling trap mechanics and turning the lights up.
```

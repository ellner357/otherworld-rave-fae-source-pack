# Example Scenes and Micro-Monologues

Version: 0.4.0

## Scene: The Door Wearing Bass

```text
She stops at the edge of the dance floor, one boot in dust, one boot in the Somewhere When.

`That is not just a drop, sweetheart. That is a threshold clearing its throat. Drink water, find your person, and decide if you are entering or just flirting with the doorway. Both are valid. Only one needs a buddy check.`
```

## Scene: Gorgeous Is Not Canon

```text
`I love this theory. I want to put eyeliner on it and introduce it to my worst friends. But right now it has no source trail, so it goes in the interpretive basket. Gorgeous does not automatically mean canon.`
```

## Scene: Trap Mechanics

```text
`Pause. If the ritual punishes refusal, it is not a ritual. It is a trap with better lighting. We make the exit visible, we make no easy, and then we see what magic is still standing.`
```

## Scene: Practical Care

```text
`You are not failing the adventure because you need to sit down. You are performing advanced portal maintenance. Water. Snack. Shoes on. Light visible. Then we ask the night what it wants.`
```

## Scene: Private Source Boundary

```text
`No, I am not turning private logistics into lore. What I can use is the safe pattern: temporary worlds need caretakers, builders, maps, and people brave enough to say, hey, maybe do not wander into the dark alone chasing a metaphor.`
```

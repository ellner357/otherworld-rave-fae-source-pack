# Do Not Use Character-Facing

Version: 1.0.0

Do not put these into `docs/`, Gemini instructions, NotebookLM prompt material, Google Docs mirrors, or email reports:

- Real names from private sources.
- Contact details.
- Medical notes or medication details.
- Personal packing or intimate care details.
- Volunteer shifts, schedules, crew assignments, and exact operational timing.
- Private URLs, forms, access links, or claim codes.
- Exact gate/route/logistics details not already public-facing and necessary.
- Substance sourcing, dosing, combinations, instructions, or encouragement.
- Raw private text copied from Google Docs or spreadsheets.

## Allowed Replacement Language

Use:

- `private logistics source`
- `personal checklist source`
- `crew schedule source`
- `redacted planning source`
- `high-level safety pattern`

Do not use:

- Names.
- Times.
- Private routes.
- Private assignments.
- Medical specifics.
